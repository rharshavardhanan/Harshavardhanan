## Measured

The ✦ in a 1280×720 frame (4px margin added), in texture terms (u across from
the left, v up from the bottom, as the ring's shader reads the picture):

| films                                          | pixels                 | u           | v           |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------- | ----------- |
| odovox, aftermeet, robot, road, kattral, cache | x 1136–1184, y 576–624 | 0.884–0.928 | 0.128–0.206 |
| halluc (its border was cropped on encoding)    | x 1172–1220, y 597–642 | 0.912–0.956 | 0.103–0.177 |

What shows of a film depends on its box: "cover" already trims the sides of a
narrow box. A ring panel (about 1.6:1) shows u 0.05–0.95; a phone's panel only
the middle third; a project page on a 16:9 or wider screen the full width. The
spec's default (zoom 1.10, shift (−0.03, +0.03)) then ends at u 0.925 on a 16:9
page: the ✦ (from 0.884) shows. **Default here: zoom 1.15, shift (−0.06,
+0.03)**: u 0.005–0.875 and v 0.095–0.965 on any box 16:9 or wider, so the mark
is out of the frame by about 12px and nothing outside the picture is ever
shown.

### Task 3: The same crop wherever a film or its poster shows

- **Goal:** The project page's film (and its poster), the menu's posters, the next-project card and the poster list crop exactly as the ring does, so the menu → page, next → page and home → page morphs do not jump.
- **How:** the box becomes a size container; the picture inside is moved and scaled by CSS: `translate(-x·zoom·W, y·zoom·H) scale(zoom)`, where W = max(100cqw, 100cqh·16/9) is the width the covering picture is drawn at (H likewise), the same sum as the shader's. The morphs' names go on the clipping box, not the picture, so they never fly out of their frame.
- **Files:** `app/globals.css`, `components/Film.tsx`, `components/Menu.tsx`, `components/Corners.tsx`, `components/NextProject.tsx`, `app/page.tsx`.

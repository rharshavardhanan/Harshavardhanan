## What changed to get here

The first measurement after the redesign was home 88 / accessibility 96, case 87.

- **Accessibility 96 to 100.** The header and hero text used "difference" blending,
  which measuring tools read as white on white. The header now switches between ink
  and paper according to the section under it (`components/HeaderTone.tsx`), and the
  hero's subtitle is ordinary text; only the giant name still inverts. Section colours
  are now hidden for their wipe only just before the section arrives, so an unscrolled
  page is measured with its real colours.
- **Performance.** GSAP, ScrollTrigger, SplitText and Lenis are fetched once the page
  has loaded and the browser is idle (`lib/after-load.ts`), and the work index fetches
  GSAP only when a mouse moves over it. The name's letters finish rising in about
  1.5 s instead of 2.

## Accessibility tests

`e2e/a11y.e2e.ts` runs axe (WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practices) on the home page in
neutral and each project's colours, on all five case studies, with the menu open, on
the 404 page and at phone width: 14 audits, no violations. The blended hero name is
checked from its real pixels in `e2e/blend.e2e.ts`.

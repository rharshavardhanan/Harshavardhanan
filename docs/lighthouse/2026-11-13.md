## Speed measures

Lighthouse can either estimate slow-phone timings from a fast run ("simulated", its
default) or actually slow the network and processor down while loading ("applied").
On a local server the estimate is pessimistic, because every file arrives at once and
is counted as needed before the first large paint.

| Page | Method | Largest paint (LCP) | Blocking time | Layout shift | Performance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Home | simulated (default) | 3.5 s | 10 ms | 0 | 90 |
| Home | applied slow 4G + 4x slower CPU | 1.8 s | 10 ms | 0 | 98 |
| Case study | simulated (default) | 3.2 s | 10 ms | 0 | 93 |
| Case study | applied slow 4G + 4x slower CPU | 1.8 s | 10 ms | 0 | 98 |

The brief's bar is a score of 90 or more and LCP under 2.5 s. The score is met either
way. LCP is met when the slowdown is actually applied (1.8 s) and not in the default
estimate (3.2 to 3.5 s).

## What was changed to get here

First run: home 81, case study 87.

- The hero's still image was hidden under the loader on phones and only appeared with
  the video, 3.6 s in. It is now the video's poster, fetched first, so it paints with
  the rest of the first screen; the video is fetched after the page has loaded.
- The recorded covers and still are small AVIF files already (11 to 27 KB), so they
  are served directly instead of through the image optimiser.
- three.js is never downloaded on phones.

## Accessibility

`e2e/a11y.e2e.ts` runs axe (WCAG 2.2 AA plus best practices) on the home page in the
neutral theme and each of the five project themes, on all five case studies, with the
menu open, on the 404 page, and at phone width: 14 audits, no violations. Two real
problems were found and fixed on the way (labels on paragraphs, which screen readers
may ignore), and the Kattral demo's tile labels were given a solid backing.

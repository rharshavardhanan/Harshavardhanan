# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Chosen in the brief: Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), Tailwind CSS, GSAP, Lenis, React
Three Fiber. Deploy target Vercel. Each library is installed in the phase that first
uses it.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers filling ML and full-stack roles, who give a
portfolio about two minutes and need to reach the work and the contact details fast.

Secondary: the Awwwards jury (Design 40%, Usability 30%, Creativity 20%, Content 10%)
and the Developer Award jury.

Confirmed 2026-11-12: when the two conflict, the recruiter wins. Spectacle never blocks
content.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Harshavardhanan Rajasimhan ("Harsha"), software engineer and
founder (AI/ML and full-stack), Chennai. Success is a recruiter contacting him about a
full-time role starting in 2027, after he graduates from VIT Chennai (reconfirmed
2026-11-12 for brief v2; Odovox is the lead proof of what he can build, not the thing
being sold). An Awwwards submission is a second goal.

## Positioning

He builds AI products end to end, from model to interface, and has founded one
(Odovox, a voice-first operating system for dental clinics). The site is a neutral
editorial shell about him as an engineer; each project then takes the page over with
its own world, a working miniature of what it does, and an honest account of where it
broke.

## Operating Context

Visited from a link in a résumé, LinkedIn or GitHub, on a work laptop or a mid-range
Android phone. Visitors skim, open one or two case studies, then look for email,
GitHub, LinkedIn and the résumé PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Site map, copy and interactions are fixed by `docs/brief.md` (v2).
- Five projects: Odovox (lead), Automated Road Damage Survey, Kattral Academy
  (Zerofence), Edge Cache RL, Hallucination Detection in LLMs. All metrics come from
  the brief and are not to be rounded up or extended.
- Odovox: never claim live clinics or users.
- The phone number never appears on the site.
- Built in six phases, reviewed after each. Harsha is learning to code and must be able
  to read and change every file.
- Undecided: the eighth Odovox module (the brief says 8, lists 7); bio text; "More
  work" rows; whether the contact form uses Resend.

## Brand Commitments

Binding, from the brief: neutral shell in ink `#0E0E0E`, paper `#F4F2EE`, grey
`#8A877F`, line `#D9D5CC`; five project themes (background / accent): Odovox
`#0F1F1C` / `#7FF2C9`, Road Damage `#121212` / `#FFC93C`, Kattral `#1C1410` /
`#FF8A3D`, Edge Cache `#0B1020` / `#4D8BFF`, Hallucination `#16111F` / `#B69CFF`;
typefaces Instrument Serif, Inter Tight, JetBrains Mono. Tone: calm, editorial, quirk in
small doses.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real project facts and copy in `docs/brief.md`.
- Live link for Edge Cache RL.
- Absent as of 2026-11-12, not to be fabricated as real: all images and videos in
  `public/media/`, and `public/resume.pdf`. Stand-ins must be visibly stand-ins.

## Product Principles

1. The recruiter's two minutes come first.
2. The shell stays quiet so the projects can be loud.
3. Show the thing working: every project has a miniature a visitor can operate.
4. Facts only: no number, user or customer the brief does not state.
5. Works without motion, without a mouse and without JavaScript.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in every theme, full keyboard navigation including every demo, visible
focus, a skip link, and full respect for `prefers-reduced-motion`. The brief names
accessibility as the place to beat past award winners.
